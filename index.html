About IGS

Institute of General Studies (IGS) offers complete coaching for Classes 5 to 12 across Science, Commerce, and Arts streams. We teach ICSE, CBSE, and State Board students in both Hindi and English medium. Our special teaching techniques include projector-based learning and map-based education for better concept clarity.
